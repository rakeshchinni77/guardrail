Exoplanet Atmospheric Spectroscopy

Published: 2026-02-28 | Author: Exoplanet Characterization Team

Observational Data

Transmission spectra for sub-Neptune TOI-270 d confirmed water vapor absorption bands at 1.4 and 1.9 micrometers.

Atmospheric metallicity was calculated at 45 times solar abundance, indicating a volatile-rich envelope.
