Solar Radiation and Photovoltaic Efficiency

Published: 2026-03-15 | Author: Dr. Aris Thorne | Laboratory for Renewable Energy

Abstract

Recent advances in perovskite-silicon tandem cells demonstrate record conversion efficiencies under standard AM1.5G solar illumination.

Key Findings

  • Perovskite-silicon tandem solar cells achieved a verified laboratory power conversion efficiency of 33.9% in early 2026.
  • The optimal top-cell bandgap for tandem architectures was determined to be 1.68 eV to maximize spectral absorption.
  • Encapsulated modules maintained 95% of initial efficiency after 1,200 hours of accelerated damp-heat testing.
  • Silicon-based monocrystalline modules accounted for 92% of global utility-scale installations in 2025.

Conclusion

Commercial deployment of tandem architectures is anticipated by late 2027 as manufacturing wafer durability reaches industrial standards.
